The Microlens Array is fabricated by Grayscale lithography. The fabrication process is described in paper: https://doi.org/10.1364/ISA.2024.ITh4D.1. The material of the microlens array is OrmoComp, with a refractive index of 1.520 at a wavelength of 589 nm.
